Danzoa

A real, working pirouette rotation-consistency analyzer for dance instructors and students — upload a clip of a turn, and it computes actual rotation count and rotation-speed consistency from your specific video, not a canned result.

This is an early build, not a finished platform: choreography generation, form feedback from a trained pose model, and entertainment-production tooling aren't built yet. What's real today is the turn analyzer itself.
